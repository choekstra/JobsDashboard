The chosen company for employment opportunities is **Cognizant** ( headquartered in Teaneck, New Jersey). Cognizant was selected due to its strategic focus on U.S. citizen roles in specialized and client-facing functions. The company's employment model supports the hiring of U.S. citizens particularly in consulting, healthcare, financial services, and transformative roles, despite its significant offshore workforce.

Companies that were not selected include:

1. **IBM** (New York) - Limited growth and high competition for roles with an emphasis on offshoring.
2. **Accenture** (Dublin, Ireland) - Similar offshore-heavy delivery model with selective U.S. hiring.
3. **Infosys** (Bengaluru, India) - Primarily hires offshore with fewer opportunities for U.S. citizens.
4. **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** (Mumbai, India) - Focused more on local hiring, but lacks the diversity of roles available to U.S. citizens compared to Cognizant.

These companies were not selected due to less favorable trends in U.S. hiring practices and their heavy reliance on offshore labor, making opportunities for U.S. citizens more competitive and limited.
